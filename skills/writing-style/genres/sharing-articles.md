# Sharing articles

Style for personal tech sharing articles (Zhihu, LinkedIn, Twitter).

## Mindset

Open-source sharing, first person, peer attitude. "I ran into X, did Y,
observed Z" style narrative.

## Tone

- Plain and direct.
- Peer attitude; use peer-to-peer phrasing (favor "I tried X and saw Y"
  over "let me explain" or "let's look at").
- Briefly pass over common knowledge; expand only where you have something
  new.

## Structure

Organize as "problem encountered, my approach, observation afterwards."
Body sections cover design goals only; means come up as implementation
detail, kept inline rather than as their own sections.

## Phrasing

- Use "kept using X, noticed Y, added Z to fix it" over "put some thought
  into it."
- Okay to call out specific engineering pain points for authenticity.

## Lines

- Prefer commas, colons, or parentheses over dashes (em dash or double
  hyphen).
