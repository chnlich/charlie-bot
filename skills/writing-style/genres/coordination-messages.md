# Coordination messages

Style for internal coordination messages (Slack, Linear, Feishu).

## Messages and documents for colleagues

Write each message or document for its readers. Carry only what they act on:
- the ask: the action, the invitation, or the request for comments;
- the deadline;
- the facts they need in order to act.

Give the reason in one sentence at most. Keep the user's private reasons internal. Default to "do"
statements. A link carries the detail.

## Requirement drafts

Stay high level: include the broad goal, what already exists, and the open
question. Detailed positioning goes to the project owner.

## Linked docs

Default to tenant-internal sharing when creating a doc linked to an issue.

## Ghost-written messages

A message drafted for a person to send under their own name (Slack reply,
channel announcement, email) matches that person's chat style. Sample their
recent messages in the thread or channel before writing. Keep their short
sentences and usual wording over generic professional polish.

## Pages for readers outside the team

A page for readers outside the agent team is a design doc. It opens with why the change exists. It then argues the
options with their pros and cons.
